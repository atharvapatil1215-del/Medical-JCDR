About Us

Salient Features of the Journal

  • Free access, peer reviewed, international biomedical journal.
  • Includes all medical and dental specialities.
  • Unique and extensive author support.
  • Regular publication every month — 12 issues per year. JCDR became monthly in August 2012.
  • Quick and easy communication between Editors and Authors.
  • Multidisciplinary journal with a wide scope.
  • JCDR Pre Publishing online submission and manuscript management system.
  • Pre-publication peer review and post-publication peer review through Reader Comments.
  • Easy download of bibliography.
  • Free full-text availability in HTML as well as PDF.
  • Email notifications on new issue release.
  • Statistics of article downloads and visits.
  • Certified by the Health on Net (HON), Geneva University Hospital.
  • Regularly crawled by major search engines including Google, Yahoo and MSN.

Genesis

There is no dearth of journals published around the world. JCDR was conceived to be different, with a focus on helping physicians and researchers who have valuable clinical experience but may lack time, motivation or scientific writing skills to publish their findings.

The journal aims to publish findings of doctors working at the ground level and those of young researchers. It also aims to help authors draft and write articles, make constructive changes to submitted drafts and provide English editorial services for authors whose second language is English.

Although the journal was started in India, it welcomes original articles and case reports from around the world and aims to provide similar unbiased service to everyone who uses its services.
